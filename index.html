Quick Answers

Before you decide

Is one format objectively better than the other?

No. It genuinely depends on your commute, your discipline, and whether you learn better with structure or flexibility.

Can I mix both formats?

Yes, and many learners do. Online for fundamentals at your own pace, offline sessions for project reviews and doubt clearing.

Does the commute really matter that much?

In a city like Mumbai, yes. A long commute can quietly make an otherwise great offline course unsustainable.
